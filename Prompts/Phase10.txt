============================================================
1. PHASE OBJECTIVE
============================================================

The objective of Phase 10 is to take the SINGLE FINAL MODEL
selected in Phase 9 and convert it into a reliable, reusable,
versioned inference package.

Phase 9 determined:

- which model is final
- why it was selected
- its performance
- its limitations

Phase 10 prepares that model for:

1. Local inference
2. Programmatic inference
3. Backend/API integration
4. React frontend integration later
5. Reproducible deployment

The model must be treated as a reusable ML service component.

============================================================

DO NOT compare models again.

DO NOT retrain all previous models.

DO NOT change the final model architecture unless a genuine
packaging/inference problem requires it.

The final model selected in Phase 9 is the model to package.
