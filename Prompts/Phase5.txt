============================================================
6. TF-IDF CONCEPT
============================================================

The implementation should support:

TF-IDF =
Term Frequency × Inverse Document Frequency

The system should use TF-IDF to assign higher importance to terms
that are useful within documents but less common across the corpus.

Do not manually implement TF-IDF unless there is a specific
educational reason.

Use a reliable library implementation.

============================================================
20. N-GRAM FEATURE ENGINEERING
============================================================

Use:

Unigram
Bigram
Trigram

The rationale:

Unigrams:
capture individual lexical information.

Bigrams:
capture local word combinations.

Trigrams:
capture short phrase-level patterns.

Sarcasm and irony may depend on combinations rather than isolated
words.

However, higher n-grams increase dimensionality.

Measure this trade-off.
