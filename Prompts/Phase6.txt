============================================================
5. WHY BI-LSTM
============================================================

The project should explain why Bi-LSTM is being used.

A traditional TF-IDF model represents text mainly through
statistical word/n-gram features.

Bi-LSTM processes sequences and can capture contextual information
from both directions.

Conceptually:

Forward LSTM:
past → present

Backward LSTM:
future → present

Bi-LSTM:
past + future context

This is useful for studying whether sequence/context information
helps distinguish:

Sarcasm
Irony
Figurative
Regular

Do not claim that Bi-LSTM automatically understands sarcasm.

The model learns statistical patterns from the training data.

============================================================
6. GLOVE
============================================================

Use pre-trained GloVe word embeddings.

GloVe provides dense vector representations for words.

Conceptually:

"great"
    ↓
[0.12, -0.31, 0.47, ...]

The embedding matrix will initialize the neural network's
word representation layer.

The GloVe embeddings must be obtained from a legitimate,
documented source.

DO NOT use another sarcasm dataset as an embedding source.

GloVe is an external pretrained language representation,
not a project classification dataset.
