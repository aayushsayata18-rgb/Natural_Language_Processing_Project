============================================================
5. WHY FastText
============================================================

Explain FastText in the project documentation.

FastText represents words using subword information.

Conceptually:

WORD
 ↓
SUBWORD N-GRAMS
 ↓
WORD REPRESENTATION

This can be useful for social-media text containing:

- misspellings
- slang
- abbreviations
- repeated characters
- informal words
- rare words
- hashtags
- morphological variations

Do NOT claim FastText automatically solves these problems.

The project must experimentally evaluate whether it helps.

============================================================
6. WHY ATTENTION
============================================================

Bi-LSTM processes sequences, but an ordinary final sequence
representation may not give equal importance to every token.

Attention allows the model to assign different importance to
different parts of the sequence.

Conceptually:

tokens
  ↓
Bi-LSTM hidden states
  ↓
attention scores
  ↓
weighted representation
  ↓
classification

This is especially interesting for sarcasm because only certain
words or phrases may strongly indicate the intended tone.

Do not claim that attention "understands" sarcasm.

It learns weights from training data.

19. ATTENTION MECHANISM
============================================================

Implement a transparent attention mechanism.

The attention layer should:

1. Receive Bi-LSTM hidden states.
2. Calculate attention scores.
3. Normalize scores.
4. Calculate weighted hidden states.
5. Produce a context representation.

Conceptually:

H = [h1, h2, ..., hn]

attention_score_i = f(hi)

attention_weight_i =
    softmax(attention_score_i)

context =
    Σ attention_weight_i * hi

Then:

context
 ↓
Dense
 ↓
Softmax

Document the mathematical formulation used.

66. ATTENTION MATHEMATICAL DOCUMENTATION
============================================================

Document the attention mechanism used.

For hidden states:

H = [h1, h2, ..., hn]

calculate:

e_i = score(h_i)

then:

α_i = exp(e_i) / Σ_j exp(e_j)

and:

context = Σ_i α_i h_i

The exact score function must match the implemented code.

Do not document equations that differ from the implementation.
