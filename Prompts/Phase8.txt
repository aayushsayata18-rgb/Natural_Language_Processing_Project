============================================================
5. WHY BERT
============================================================

The documentation must explain BERT conceptually.

BERT is a transformer-based pretrained language model that
produces contextual representations.

Unlike traditional:

BoW
TF-IDF

the meaning of a token can be represented in relation to its
surrounding context.

BERT is also different from a standard one-directional sequence
representation because it uses bidirectional contextual
information through Transformer attention.

For this project, BERT is useful because sarcasm and irony often
depend on:

- context
- word relationships
- contradiction
- phrase meaning
- surrounding words
- subtle linguistic patterns

Do NOT claim that BERT inherently "understands" sarcasm.

It is being fine-tuned to learn patterns associated with the four
classes.
